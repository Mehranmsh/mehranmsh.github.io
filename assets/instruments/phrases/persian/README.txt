MEHRAN MUSIC Persian recorded phrase previews

These are short real-performance excerpts (four per instrument), not isolated notes or full chromatic multisample banks.
Intended use: one-shot phrase playback in MEHRAN MUSIC. Do not pitch-shift them and label them as playable multisampled instruments.

Dataset: Persian Classical Music Instrument Recognition (PCMIR) Database
DOI: 10.17632/bpyxwkv9wj.1
