Free AI image generation and photo editing
FLUX MODELS

Create from text with FLUX.1 SCHNELL, or upload a reference photo and add a prompt to edit and recreate the scene with FLUX.2 KLEIN 9B. Your reference photo is uploaded privately and processed by Cloudflare Workers AI. No external paid model or paid fallback is used. Both modes share Cloudflare's daily free AI allocation. On a Workers Paid account, usage above the free daily allowance can be billed; use Workers Free to avoid overage charges.
